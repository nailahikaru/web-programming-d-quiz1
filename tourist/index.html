Surabaya tourist attractions

Lets go on an adventure!

1. Tugu Pahlawan

This iconic monument, standing 41.15 meters tall, was built to commemorate the bravery of Surabaya's residents in the Battle of November 10, 1945. In its underground area, there is the Museum 10 November, which displays interactive dioramas, historical relics, and original recordings of Bung Tomo's speech.

2. Submarine Monument

Monkasel displays the original Soviet-made submarine KRI Pasopati 410, which once strengthened the Indonesian Navy's fleet. Visitors can enter the interior of the submarine to see the control room, periscope, and even the torpedo room.

3. Suroboyo Bridge

A bridge connecting the coastal area of Surabaya at Kenjeran Beach, inaugurated on July 9, 2016. This bridge is 800 meters long, 18 meters wide, and 12 meters high, supported by 150 piles. This bridge was designed directly by Surabaya Mayor Tri Rismaharini. The bridge's design is built in a curved shape with a fountain view in the center.
