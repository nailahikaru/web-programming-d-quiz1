Surabaya

I'm from a very cool city (not the temperature tho) called Surabaya. Surabaya is the capital of East Java and Indonesia's second-largest metropolis, Surabaya serves as a vital commercial, educational, and maritime hub for eastern Indonesia. Affectionately known as the "City of Heroes" (Kota Pahlawan) due to its pivotal role on November 10, 1945, in the Battle of Surabaya during the Indonesian National Revolution, this historic event also inspired the name of my campu (Institut Teknologi Sepuluh Nopember). Situated along Java's northern coast on the Madura Strait. It houses Tanjung Perak, one of the nation's busiest seaports and connects to Madura Island via the 5.4-kilometer Suramadu Bridge. Today, Surabaya features a vibrant landscape of contemporary skyscrapers, well-maintained green parks, and preserved Dutch colonial architecture, all paired with a celebrated local food culture famous for rich, savory East Javanese dishes like Lontong Balap and Rujak Cingur.

According to local folklore, the city's name and iconic emblem originate from a legendary battle between a white shark named Sura and a crocodile named Baya, who fought endlessly for dominance over prey in the region. After agreeing to split their territories, Sura in the ocean and Baya on land, Sura broke the pact by hunting up local rivers, sparking a fierce duel that ended with Sura severely bitten and fleeing back to the sea. Linguistically combining sura (braving danger) and baya (danger) to reflect the courageous spirit of its inhabitants, this myth inspired the city's official coat of arms and its famous landmark statue depicting the two creatures locked in combat.
